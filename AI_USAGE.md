# AI Usage Policy

## Purpose

AI tools may be used as development assistants during the PCSE
Hardware research project.

AI assistance is treated as a productivity and documentation tool,
not as a substitute for engineering validation or scientific judgment.

## Permitted Uses

AI tools may assist with:

- SystemVerilog development
- Python scripting
- C and RISC-V assembly development
- Shell commands
- Debugging
- Documentation
- Testbench construction
- Data-processing scripts
- Code review
- Refactoring suggestions
- Research-paper language editing
- Explanations of technical concepts

## Validation Requirement

AI-generated code must be reviewed and tested by the project author
before being used in experiments.

AI-generated scientific claims must be independently checked against
primary sources or authoritative documentation.

AI-generated numerical results are not considered experimental
evidence until reproduced by the project's own experimental workflow.

## Disclosure

Substantive AI assistance is recorded in:

- `ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md`
- `docs/AI_PROMPTS.md`

## Research Responsibility

The project author remains responsible for:

- correctness of RTL
- correctness of experiments
- interpretation of results
- citations
- scientific claims
- final manuscript content
